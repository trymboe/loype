# Planning (`planner@1`)

Plans are generated by deterministic code in `src/lib/planning/` from your goal, your availability and a **baseline** (fitness estimate + what you actually ran in the last 4–8 weeks). No LLM is involved. Every workout stores *why* it is there.

## Baseline (`baseline.ts`)
- Weekly minutes, km and runs: average of the last 28 days.
- Consistency: weeks with at least one run, out of the last 8.
- Longest run: last 8 weeks.
- Fitness: VDOT, pace zones, HR zones and race predictions from the fitness model. Without an estimate, targets are HR-only; without HR zones, effort descriptions. Paces are never invented.

## Generator (`generator.ts`)
- **Length**: weeks to the race (max 24; if further away, the plan starts 24 weeks out), else the chosen length.
- **Phases**: taper weeks including race week: 5k/10k 1, half 2, marathon 3. Of the rest, ~30 % race-specific, ~35 % build, base first. A recovery week every 4th week (every 3rd when consistency < 60 %), never just before the taper. A distance goal without a race date ends with a time trial.
- **Volume**: starts at your recent weekly time (min 20 min per run day). Grows by the smaller of 10 % (6 % with low consistency) or 30 min per week, half that after a recovery week. Peak cap by goal (5k 4 h, 10k 5 h, half 6 h, marathon 8 h) and your own limit. Recovery weeks 70 %; taper 60 % (1 wk), 75/50 % (2), 80/60/40 % (3) of peak.
- **Long run**: 27–30 % of the week, at least ~80 % of your recent longest run (bounded by 45 % of the week), grows at most 5–10 min per week, capped at 75/90/120/150 min (5k/10k/half/marathon).
- **Quality sessions**: 2 with ≥ 4 run days, 1 with 3, none with 2; placed ≥ 2 days from each other and the long run.
- **Session types by phase**: base: tempo / hills + strides; build: threshold + VO2max (5k/10k/general) or threshold + tempo/VO2max (half/marathon); race-specific: race-pace + threshold/reps, long runs with goal-pace segments for half/marathon; taper: short race-pace + strides; race week: primer with strides, rest the day before.
- **Race pace**: target pace if within reach (≤ 0.6 %/week improvement, max 8 %) of the current prediction; otherwise the reachable pace, with a warning. Without a target: predicted pace.

## Matching and execution (`match.ts`)
- A run matches a planned workout on the same day (score 1) or ±1 day (0.6), minus a duration mismatch penalty, ± 0.1 for hard/easy agreement. Threshold 0.4; best pairs first. Unmatched runs stay unscheduled.
- A planned workout is **missed** once 2 days have passed without a match.
- Execution: work pace = your best effort whose duration is closest to one work bout, compared with the target range (±3 %); easy runs are checked against the easy HR ceiling; duration ratio < 75 % or > 130 % is noted.

## Adaptation (`adapt.ts`) — proposals only
| Rule | Trigger | Change |
|---|---|---|
| A | Fitness estimate moved ≥ 1 VDOT (medium/high confidence), or ≥ 2 of the last 3 quality sessions ≥ 3 % faster than target with RPE ≤ 7 | Re-target future paces (structure unchanged) |
| B | ≥ 2 missed sessions in 10 days and next week planned > 110 % of last 7 days' running + 30 min | Shorten non-key runs in the next 7 days; missed sessions are not made up |
| C | Easy run rated RPE ≥ 8 in the last 3 days, or legs heavy/sore in 2 of the last 3 check-ins | Next hard session within 3 days becomes an easy run |
| D | No run for ≥ 12 days | Next 7 days at ~55 %, no hard sessions |

Proposals are deduplicated by a key, expire when they would change the past, never touch locked or finished workouts, and can be accepted, declined ("Keep my plan") or undone. Changing the goal builds a new plan from today and archives the old one with its history.

## Versions
Every generation, edit, accepted proposal and undo writes a full snapshot to `plan_versions`. Any version can be restored from Plan → History (finished workouts are left as they are).

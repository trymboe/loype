# Planning (`planner@1`)

Plans are generated by deterministic code in `src/lib/planning/` from your goal, your availability and a **baseline** (fitness estimate + what you actually ran in the last 4–8 weeks). No LLM is involved. Every workout stores *why* it is there.

## Baseline (`baseline.ts`)
- Weekly minutes, km and runs: average of the last 28 days.
- Consistency: weeks with at least one run, out of the last 8.
- Longest run: last 8 weeks.
- Fitness: VDOT, pace zones, HR zones and race predictions from the fitness model. Without an estimate, targets are HR-only; without HR zones, effort descriptions. Paces are never invented.

## Generator (`generator.ts`)
- **Days vs frequency**: the days you tick are *availability*, not a schedule. How many of them you run comes from your load: runs average ~45 min (longer runs before more runs), at least 2 a week, and at most one more than the most you ran in a week over the last 4 weeks, plus one every three weeks into the plan. Runs are spread around the week, keeping your long-run day; other days are rest days, and moving a session onto an unticked day warns you.
- **Weekly refresh**: at the first sync of each new week, the rest of the plan (this week onward) is recalculated from what you actually ran: last week, your 4-week average, your most runs in a week, and your longest run. If you handled more, it grows; if you did less, it backs off. Phases and the race date stay; locked, moved, swapped, finished and skipped workouts and accepted adaptations are kept; pending suggestions are retired; a version is saved, so it can be restored from History.
- **Length**: weeks to the race (max 24; if further away, the plan starts 24 weeks out), else the chosen length.
- **Phases**: taper weeks including race week: 5k/10k 1, half 2, marathon 3. Of the rest, ~30 % race-specific, ~35 % build, base first. A recovery week (70 %) every 4th week (every 3rd when consistency < 60 %), never just before the taper. A distance goal without a race date ends with a time trial.
- **Load limits (injury-aware)**: the first week starts from the larger of last week and your 4-week average, plus three quarters of one safe step, and never above 1.3× the 4-week average (+10 min). Each week grows by at most 10 % (at least 10, at most 30 min; 6 %/6–20 min with patchy training), half that after a recovery week, and never above 1.3× the average of the 4 weeks before it (acute:chronic ratio). A week's planned sessions must stay within 10 % of its target: easy runs, then the long run, then hard sessions are shortened or dropped to fit. Peak caps by goal (5k 4 h, 10k 5 h, half 6 h, marathon 8 h) and your own limit.
- **Intensity (80/20)**: at most 20 % of a week's time at threshold or harder (minimum allowance 12 min); sessions are shortened, then dropped, to fit. Hard sessions: 2 with ≥ 4 runs, 1 with 3, none with 2; at most 1 in base weeks; none in the first two weeks when you run under 90 min a week. Hard sessions are ≥ 2 days from each other and the long run, and the day after one is a short recovery run.
- **Long run**: ~30 % of the week (more when you run 2–3 times), at least ~80 % of your recent longest run (bounded by 40 % of the week), grows at most 5 min a week, capped at 75/90/120/150 min (5k/10k/half/marathon).
- **Session types by phase**: base: tempo / hills + strides; build: threshold + VO2max (5k/10k/general) or threshold + tempo/VO2max (half/marathon); race-specific: race-pace + threshold/reps, long runs with goal-pace segments for half/marathon; taper: short race-pace + strides; race week: short easy runs, a primer with strides, rest the day before.

## Projection (`projection.ts`)
"If you follow this plan: 10 km X → Y". A heuristic, labelled as an estimate:
- Typical VDOT gain per training week by level: < 40: 0.30, < 45: 0.25, < 50: 0.20, < 55: 0.15, < 60: 0.10, else 0.06.
- Diminishing returns: effective weeks = 16 · (1 − e^(−weeks/16)).
- Volume factor: 0.7 + 0.3 · log2(plan average ÷ your recent weekly time), clamped to 0.5–1.2.
- Total gain capped at 12 % of VDOT. Range: 0.4× to 1.3× the gain. Endurance correction uses the longest planned long run.
- Updated on the Plan page from your current fitness estimate, the remaining training weeks and your completion rate over the last 4 weeks (0.5–1), and compared with the projection at the start.
- Race pace for race-specific sessions is your target pace when it is within the optimistic end of the projection; otherwise the projected pace, with a warning. Without a target, it is your current predicted pace (re-targeted by rule A as fitness changes).

## Matching and execution (`match.ts`)
- A run matches a planned workout on the same day (score 1) or ±1 day (0.6), minus a duration mismatch penalty, ± 0.1 for hard/easy agreement. Threshold 0.4; best pairs first. Unmatched runs stay unscheduled.
- A planned workout is **missed** once 2 days have passed without a match.
- Execution: work pace = your best effort whose duration is closest to one work bout, compared with the target range (±3 %); easy runs are checked against the easy HR ceiling; duration ratio < 75 % or > 130 % is noted.

## Adaptation (`adapt.ts`) — proposals only
| Rule | Trigger | Change |
|---|---|---|
| A | Fitness estimate moved ≥ 1 VDOT (medium/high confidence), or ≥ 2 of the last 3 quality sessions ≥ 3 % faster than target with RPE ≤ 7 | Re-target future paces (structure unchanged) |
| B | ≥ 2 missed sessions in 10 days and next week planned > 110 % of last 7 days' running + 30 min | Shorten non-key runs in the next 7 days; missed sessions are not made up |
| C | Easy run rated RPE ≥ 8 in the last 3 days, or legs heavy/sore in 2 of the last 3 check-ins | Next hard session within 3 days becomes an easy run |
| D | No run for ≥ 12 days | Next 7 days at ~55 %, no hard sessions |

Proposals are deduplicated by a key, expire when they would change the past, never touch locked or finished workouts, and can be accepted, declined ("Keep my plan") or undone. Changing the goal builds a new plan from today and archives the old one with its history.

## Versions
Every generation, edit, accepted proposal and undo writes a full snapshot to `plan_versions`. Any version can be restored from Plan → History (finished workouts are left as they are).
